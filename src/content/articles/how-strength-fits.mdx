<p>Your marathon plan already writes "rest + strength" on Wednesday and Friday. Those are the right two days, but they are not interchangeable, and the reason matters more than it looks.</p>

<div class="week">
<div class="day"><h5>Mon</h5><p>Easy run<span class="sub">Hip block after</span></p></div>
<div class="day"><h5>Tue</h5><p>Quality<span class="sub">Hardest run of the week</span></p></div>
<div class="day day--lift"><h5>Wed</h5><p><strong>Session A</strong><span class="sub">Lower + vertical pull · the heavy one</span></p></div>
<div class="day"><h5>Thu</h5><p>Medium-long<span class="sub">Hip block after</span></p></div>
<div class="day day--lift"><h5>Fri</h5><p><strong>Session B</strong><span class="sub">Upper + hips · legs stay fresh</span></p></div>
<div class="day"><h5>Sat</h5><p>Long run<span class="sub">Nothing may compromise this</span></p></div>
<div class="day day--rest"><h5>Sun</h5><p>Recovery run<span class="sub">Hip block, nothing else</span></p></div>
</div>

<p><strong>All heavy leg work lives on Wednesday.</strong> It sits 24 hours after the quality session, when you're already fatigued and nothing is at stake, and a full 48 hours before Thursday's medium-long run. It is also as far from Saturday as the week allows.</p>

<p><strong>Friday is deliberately light on legs.</strong> This is the correction most people miss. Loading the legs on Friday puts peak muscle soreness — which arrives 24 to 48 hours later — directly on top of the most important run of your week. One heavy Friday squat session can quietly ruin four consecutive Saturdays before you work out why the long runs feel bad. So Friday is chest, back, arms, core and hips: everything that makes you stronger without making Saturday worse.</p>

<p>Does one heavy leg session a week build legs? Not much. But it holds strength very well — maintaining strength needs far less volume than gaining it did, provided the <em>intensity</em> stays high. That is why the leg work in this plan is heavy and brief rather than moderate and long. Sets of five hold what sets of twelve cannot.</p>
